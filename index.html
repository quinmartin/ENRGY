How the score works

Each app's score comes from the CPU time it used, measured against everything your Mac's cores could have done in that time. Small amounts of work still show up, and only the really heavy apps reach the top of the scale.

Installing

  1. Download the zip and double-click it to unzip.
  2. Drag EnergyPercentages into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. If macOS says it can't check the app, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. To see scores on your Dock, allow EnergyPercentages under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

The warning in step 3 appears because this app is shared directly instead of through the Mac App Store. You only need to approve it once.
